Test details

Number of items

Class MPS and item MPS are compared to this. DepEd's usual target is 75.

Answer key

Type the letters in order, like ABDCA BBCDA. Spaces and numbers are ignored. Use a dash for an item that isn't scored. Only fill the sets you used.

Class list (fills in names)

One student per line: class number, then name, like 12 Dela Cruz, Juan. Names go to sheets in the section chosen on the Scan sheets page, now and for sheets you scan later.

Scan answer sheets

Sheets you scan next are filed under this section. Leave it empty if you only have one section.

You can also drop photos here. It works out on its own whether the sheet has 30, 50 or 60 items.

    • Lay the sheet flat in good light, with no shadow across it.
    • Keep all four black corner squares inside the photo.
    • Any angle works, even sideways or upside down.
