Printable answer sheets

The same blank sheet works for every test. Students shade their answers; you type the answer key into the app. Choose the number of items, then print.

Number of items

Giving a test with fewer items, say 40? Use the 50-item sheet and set the test to 40 items in SagotScan. Items after 40 are ignored.

Before you print

  1. Print at 100% (actual size) on plain white paper, in black. Turn off "fit to page" if your printer offers it. Any scale still scans, but standard size gives the best marks.
  2. All four black corner squares must print fully. Do not trim the page edges.
  3. To make a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  4. Students use a No. 2 pencil or a dark ballpoint pen and fill each circle completely.
